## What Works

- Verified run evidence is visibly separated from concept previews.
- The serif and sans-serif pairing and generous section rhythm support the long page's hierarchy.
- The loop's desktop SVG communicates the cycle and remains appropriate at wider widths.

## Main Weakness

The evolution-loop section is awkward to read on narrow screens because the desktop SVG overflows the viewport and requires horizontal scrolling. The existing vertical sequence is not being used as a deliberate mobile presentation.

## Root Cause

The responsive rule enlarges the SVG to 720px and translates it left inside an overflow-scrolling figure. Meanwhile, the text sequence has no display or spacing rules, so it is visible at desktop and does not replace the oversized diagram on mobile.

## Design Lesson

When a diagram cannot preserve legibility at phone width, use a purpose-built linear representation on small screens and keep the spatial diagram for larger viewports. A responsive alternative should replace the constrained view and remain accessible as an equivalent description.
